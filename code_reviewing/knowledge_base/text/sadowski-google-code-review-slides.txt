Discussion points
§ Is Google’s one-approver norm (less than other companies) mainly viable because
  ownership and readability create clear accountability, and would it still hold in
  teams without those guardrails or in tightly coupled code?
§ The paper suggests very large diffs often include mass deletes but they didn't
  mention why; why should these be handled differently?
§ Would an employee’s geographic location and time zone meaningfully affect their
  satisfaction?
§ Could LLMs automate enough to allow guarded auto-merge for narrow, well-
  tested changes, or will human reviewers always be required for design, security,
  and accountability?
