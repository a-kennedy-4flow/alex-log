                                              Discussion: LIRTI2 is an out-of-school, small-group intervention with potential
                                              scalability in low-resource settings where access to services is limited. Reading
                                              fluency and comprehension improved following the intervention, with larger
                                              gains in children with ADHD than in those with BIF or arDYS. Future studies
                                              with follow-up are needed to confirm which learner profiles benefit most and
                                              to determine the intervention’s broader academic impact.
