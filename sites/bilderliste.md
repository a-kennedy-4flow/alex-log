# Photographs to buy

Sixty photographs cover the whole set. Two per business. A hero band across the top and
one supporting picture beside the text. A further twenty five are listed at the end for
the pages that hold galleries.

Portraits of people are not on this list. Those are drawn by `assets/bildnisse.py` and
need no licence.

## Rules that apply to every picture

- **Hero** is landscape at 1600 by 900 or larger. **Tafel** is landscape at 900 by 675 or
  larger. Buy the biggest size the licence includes and let the file be scaled down.
- **No recognisable faces.** Every business here is invented. A real person shown as an
  employee of a business that does not exist breaks the usual stock licence and misleads
  the reader. Hands and backs and over the shoulder shots are fine.
- **No logos and no number plates and no shop signs in English.** The town is German.
- **Central European look.** German signage or none. Deciduous trees rather than palms.
- **Match the season the page names.** The Mosterei presses in autumn. The Imkerei
  harvests in May and July.
- Save each file under the exact name below into `assets/bilder/`. It replaces the
  generated placeholder and no page needs editing.

## What it costs

Sixty images at the prices I checked earlier. One month of Envato Elements at about
sixteen dollars covers all sixty. Depositphotos on demand comes to roughly one hundred
and twenty dollars. Adobe Stock would be about one hundred and eighty on the ten a month
plan spread over six months.

## After downloading

1. Drop the files into `assets/bilder/` under the exact names above.
2. Run `python3 assets/aufnahmen.py` to take fresh screenshots.
3. Run `python3 assets/vorlagen.py` to rebuild the library.

Only the Apotheke currently shows a hero and a Tafel in its markup. Tell me when the
files are in place and I will wire the other twenty nine pages to their pictures.
