What it is

Riboflavin, also known as vitamin B2, is a vitamin found in food and sold as a dietary supplement. It is essential to the formation of two major coenzymes, flavin mononucleotide and flavin adenine dinucleotide. These coenzymes are involved in energy metabolism, cellular respiration, and antibody production, as well as normal growth and development.

The figure a day becomes a figure per 100 ml on the 793,4 ml a baby of 1-3.49 mo was measured drinking.

The adequate intake for this age was itself calculated from an assumed breast milk composition times an assumed intake of 0,78 litres a day. A gap between the milk and the figure is evidence about the assumption rather than about the milk. Breast milk is the reference for what adequate means at this age so a figure under the intake is a question about the intake.

Depending on the vitamin in question, the studies used for setting the AIs included as few as 5 women (riboflavin) and no more than 111 women (vitamin B12) and were conducted between 1951 and 1997. Thus, the current AIs are based on data from outdated studies with few participants, indicating the need for revision.

How much is absorbed

No measurement of how much of this is absorbed from either milk is carried here. A concentration on this page is what is in the milk rather than what reaches the child.

What to watch

  • Breast milk delivers 29% of the adequate intake. That is not a shortfall in the milk. The adequate intake for this age was itself calculated from an assumed breast milk composition times an assumed intake of 0,78 litres a day. A gap between the milk and the figure is evidence about the assumption rather than about the milk. The milk of a healthy mother is what adequate means at this age.
