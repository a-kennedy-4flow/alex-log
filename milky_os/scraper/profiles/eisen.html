What it is

Iron is a chemical element; it has symbol Fe and atomic number 26. It is a metal that belongs to the first transition series and group 8 of the periodic table. It is, by mass, the most common element on Earth, forming much of Earth's outer and inner core.

The figure a day becomes a figure per 100 ml on the 793,4 ml a baby of 1-3.49 mo was measured drinking.

The adequate intake for this age was itself calculated from an assumed breast milk composition times an assumed intake of 0,78 litres a day. A gap between the milk and the figure is evidence about the assumption rather than about the milk. Breast milk is the reference for what adequate means at this age so a figure under the intake is a question about the intake.

Depending on the vitamin in question, the studies used for setting the AIs included as few as 5 women (riboflavin) and no more than 111 women (vitamin B12) and were conducted between 1951 and 1997. Thus, the current AIs are based on data from outdated studies with few participants, indicating the need for revision.

How much is absorbed

Absorbed from human milk47%

Measured by stable iron isotope dilution from birth to 6 months.

Breastfed infants took in 0,27 mg a day and absorbed 0,128. Formula fed infants took in 11,19 mg a day and absorbed 0,457. Formula carries 41 times the iron and delivers 3,6 times the absorbed iron.

What to watch

  • A concentration here is not what reaches the child. 47% of this is absorbed from human milk against 4,1% from a powder, so a step carrying many times the milk does not deliver many times as much. Breastfed infants took in 0,27 mg a day and absorbed 0,128. Formula fed infants took in 11,19 mg a day and absorbed 0,457. Formula carries 41 times the iron and delivers 3,6 times the absorbed iron.
  • Breast milk delivers 74,1% of the adequate intake. That is not a shortfall in the milk. The adequate intake for this age was itself calculated from an assumed breast milk composition times an assumed intake of 0,78 litres a day. A gap between the milk and the figure is evidence about the assumption rather than about the milk. The milk of a healthy mother is what adequate means at this age.
