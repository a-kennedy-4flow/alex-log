What it is

Folate, also known as vitamin B9 and folacin, is one of the B vitamins. Folate is required for the body to make DNA and RNA and metabolize amino acids necessary for cell division and maturation of blood cells. As the human body cannot make folate, it is required in the diet, making it an essential nutrient.

What to watch

  • A pack printing this is counted under Folat on every chart so no chart draws this name on its own. Folic acid is a folate so the total is taken wherever a pack prints both. Human milk carries natural folate and never folic acid so without this the comparison reads as though breast milk declared no folate at all. This page is here so the name still leads somewhere.
  • Breast milk has no figure for 3 of the ten lactation windows because the study behind it does not reach them: 4. bis 17. Tag, 7. bis 8. Monat, 8. bis 8,5. Monat.
  • The reference figure does not change with the month because its source publishes one number for mature milk rather than a curve.
