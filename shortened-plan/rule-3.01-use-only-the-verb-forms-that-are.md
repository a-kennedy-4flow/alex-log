# Rule 3.1 — Verb forms and tenses of verbs

*ASD-STE100 Issue 9 (2025-01-15), part 1 - Writing rules, Section 3 – Verbs, page 1-3-1*

> Use only the verb forms that are given in the dictionary.

The STE dictionary gives you the verb forms that you can use for each approved verb.

Example:

REMOVE (v)

REMOVES REMOVED, REMOVED

GIVE (v)

GIVES GAVE, GIVEN

The introduction to the dictionary in part 2 gives you more information about the verb forms
