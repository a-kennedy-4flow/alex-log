# Rule 8.7 — Hyphenated words

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-8-8)
> Hyphenated words count as one word.

- **Applies to:** the word count, and to the three-word limits of rules 2.1 and 1.9.
- **Needs:** text.
- **Decidable:** yes.
- **Code:** `Ste.Text` keeps a hyphenated group as one token, and
  `Ste.Text.hyphen_parts/1` gives its parts for the vocabulary rules.

## Discrete form

```
ste_word_count(sentence):
    a group of words connected by hyphens counts as 1 word

rule 1.1 exception:
    a hyphenated group is permitted when the whole group is in the dictionary or
    the glossary, or when every part of it is
```

## Examples with their counts

| Text | Count |
| --- | --- |
| `Clean the surface with a soap-and-water solution.` | 7 |
| `Use the trial-and-error method.` | 4 |
| `Cutoff-switch power connection` | 3 |
| `Main-gear-door retraction-winch handle` | 3 |

## The interaction with rule 2.1

Hyphens are the standard's second method for making a long multi-word noun obey the
three-word limit (rule 2.2), so this counter rule is what makes that method work.
The limit that remains is the one that rule 8.2 states: do not put more than three
words in one hyphenated group, and do not connect words that are not related.
`Main-gear-door-retraction-winch handle` counts as two words and is still wrong.

## Notes

- A dash written as a hyphen with a space on each side is not a hyphenated word.
  Split on `-` only when there is no space on either side.
