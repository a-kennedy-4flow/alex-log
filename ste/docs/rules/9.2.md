# Rule 9.2 — Use each approved word correctly

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-9-5)
> Use each approved word correctly.

- **Applies to:** every approved word of the text.
- **Needs:** dictionary (approved meaning and part of speech), judgment.
- **Decidable:** partly. It is the combination of rule 1.2 (part of speech) and
  rule 1.3 (approved meaning), stated for the writer instead of for the dictionary.
- **Code:** not implemented; implement 1.2 and 1.3 and let this rule reference them.

## Discrete form

```
check(text, dictionary D, restricted R) -> [finding]

findings = 1.3(text, D, R)          # the approved meaning
        ++ 1.2(text, D)             # the approved part of speech

pass = findings == []
```

## The two halves, with the examples of the standard

### Restricted meaning (rule 1.3)

| Not STE | STE | Approved meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Wear protective clothing.` | `Put on protective clothing.` | wear = to become damaged by friction |
| `This regulation extends to all units.` | `This regulation is applicable to all units.` | extend = to increase in dimension or range |
| `When the pressure goes down, lift the cover.` | `When the pressure decreases, lift the cover.` | go down = physical movement of an indicator |
| `Make sure that the probe went through more than 500 °C.` | `Make sure that the temperature of the probe increased to more than 500 °C.` | go through = to move through something |
| `Move the tube to see if the inner connection is tight.` | `Move the tube to make sure that the inner connection is tight.` | see = to see with your eyes |
| `The indicator turns green.` | `The color of the indicator changes to green.` | turn = to move around an axis or a point |
| `Do not let the pressure go below 20 psi.` | `Do not let the pressure become less than 20 psi.` | below = physical position |

### Approved part of speech (rule 1.2)

| Not STE | STE | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `When you work with cleaning agents, use breathing equipment.` | `When you do work with cleaning agents, ...` | "work" is a noun, not a verb |
| `Install the cover with the help of a second person.` | `Install the cover with the aid of a second person.` | "help" is a verb, not a noun |
| `Be careful not to damage the sleeve.` | `Be careful not to cause damage to the sleeve.` | "damage" is a noun, not a verb |

## Words with two parts of speech

A small number of approved words have more than one part of speech and more than one
meaning, and their position in the sentence shows which is meant:

- `Flush the pipes with a disinfectant solution.` — verb, "to remove something or to
  operate with a flow of liquid".
- `Make sure that the door is flush with the adjacent surfaces.` — adjective, "one
  surface fully touches a different surface".

`Ste.Dictionary.approved_as/1` returns both, which is what a checker needs in order
not to report either sentence.
