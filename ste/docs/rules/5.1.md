# Rule 5.1 — Sentence length in procedures

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-5-1)
> Write short sentences. Use a maximum of 20 words in each sentence.

- **Applies to:** every sentence of a procedure, including warnings, cautions, and
  other safety instructions. Notes are the exception: rule 5.5 gives them 25 words.
- **Needs:** text, structure (procedure or description, note or work step).
- **Decidable:** yes, once the word count of rules 8.4 to 8.7 is implemented.
- **Code:** not implemented. `Ste.Text` implements the tokenizing half of 8.6 and
  8.7.

## Discrete form

```
check(text, structure S) -> [finding]

for each sentence s of S where S.mode(s) == :procedural:
    limit = 25 if S.kind(s) == :note else 20
    n = ste_word_count(s)
    if n > limit: finding(s, count: n, limit: limit)

pass = findings == []

ste_word_count: rule 8.5 (parentheses = 1 word), rule 8.6 (numbers, numbers with
units, abbreviations, alphanumeric identifiers, quoted text, titles and labels,
proper nouns of individuals, groups, organizations, and geopolitical entities =
1 word each), rule 8.7 (a hyphenated group = 1 word), rule 8.4 (a colon in a
vertical list ends the sentence). Numbers that identify a paragraph or a work
step are not counted at all.
```

## Examples with their counts

| Verdict | Text | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pass | `Install the three auxiliary screws (2) in the flange of the motor assembly (9).` | 14 |
| pass | `CAUTION: WHEN YOU REMOVE THE SHROUD (26), BE CAREFUL NOT TO CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE SURFACE OF THE FLANGE ASSEMBLY (22).` | 20 |
| fail | `Put preservation oil into the unit through the vent hole until the oil level is approximately 6 mm (0.24 inches) below the surface of the flange cover.` | 25 → two sentences of 10 and 16 |

## Notes

- The repair is not always two work steps: in the failing example the two actions
  occur at the same time, so the sentence is split into two sentences inside one
  work step (rule 5.2).
- A safety instruction obeys this rule too, which is easy to miss because its text
  is in uppercase letters and its lines look like a block.
- The note limit of 25 words is the same number as the descriptive limit of rule
  6.3, because a note is descriptive text inside a procedure.
