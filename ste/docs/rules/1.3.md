# Rule 1.3 — Approved meaning

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-1-4)
> Use approved words only with their approved meanings.

- **Applies to:** every approved word of the text.
- **Needs:** dictionary (approved meanings), judgment.
- **Decidable:** partly. Word sense is not decidable from the string alone; a
  list of the collocations that the standard itself rejects catches the frequent
  errors.
- **Code:** not implemented.

## Discrete form

```
check(text, dictionary D, restricted R) -> [finding]

for each word w of text that D approves:
    if (w, its context) matches a pattern in R:  finding(w, approved_meaning: D.meaning(w))

pass = findings == []
```

`R` is a list of "word + context → not the approved meaning" patterns, taken
from the examples of rules 1.3 and 9.2 and from the dictionary help. It grows as
a project finds more; it never becomes complete, so a pass of this rule means
"no known misuse", not "the meaning is right".

## Known patterns from the standard

| Word | Approved meaning | Rejected use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| follow | come after, go after | `Follow the safety instructions.` → `Obey the safety instructions.` |
| wear | to become damaged by friction | `Wear protective clothing.` → `Put on protective clothing.` |
| extend | to increase in dimension or range | `This regulation extends to all units.` → `is applicable to` |
| go down | physical movement of an indicator | `When the pressure goes down` → `When the pressure decreases` |
| go through | to move through something | `the probe went through 500 °C` → `the temperature increased to more than 500 °C` |
| see | to see with your eyes | `Move the tube to see if the connection is tight.` → `to make sure that` |
| turn | to move around an axis or a point | `The indicator turns green.` → `The color of the indicator changes to green.` |
| above, below | physical position | `Do not let the pressure go below 20 psi.` → `become less than` |

## Out of scope

- The part of speech of the word — rule 1.2.
- A verb plus a preposition that makes a new meaning — rule 9.3 (phrasal verbs).

## Notes

- The finding should print the approved meaning from the dictionary, because that
  is what tells the writer why the sentence is wrong.
- This rule and rule 9.2 overlap: 9.2 is the "how to use approved words
  correctly" statement of the same requirement and adds the part-of-speech half.
  Keep 1.3 for meaning and 1.2 for part of speech, and let 9.2 reference both.
