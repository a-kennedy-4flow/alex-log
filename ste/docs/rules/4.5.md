# Rule 4.5 — Articles and demonstrative adjectives

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-4-8)
> When applicable, use an article (the, a, an) or a demonstrative adjective (this,
> these) before a noun or a multi-word noun.

- **Applies to:** every noun and multi-word noun.
- **Needs:** parser, judgment (the exceptions are semantic).
- **Decidable:** heuristic. "When applicable" carries four exceptions that need
  the meaning of the sentence.
- **Code:** not implemented.

## Discrete form

```
check(text) -> [finding]

for each noun phrase n of text with no article and no demonstrative adjective:
    if n is a general statement or a concept:            continue   # exception
    if n is an abstract quality:                         continue   # exception
    if n is a later item of a series that has one article: continue  # exception
    if n is a proper noun followed by an alphanumeric identifier: continue
    if n is a title, heading, quoted text, or label:     continue
    else: finding(n, :article_omitted)

for each noun phrase n with a definite article and an alphanumeric identifier:
    finding(n, :article_before_identifier)

pass = findings == []
```

## The exceptions, from the standard

| Exception | Example in STE |
| --- | --- |
| general statement or concept | `Solvents can cause damage to paint.` |
| general concept after a preposition | `The machine operates with high efficiency.` |
| abstract quality | `This software increases performance.` |
| a series of items where one article covers all of them | `Discard the O-rings (3), gaskets (4), seals (7), and washers (9).` |
| a proper noun that an alphanumeric identifier makes definite | `Tag circuit breaker 36L7.` (`the circuit breaker 36L7` is incorrect) |

## Examples

| Verdict | Text | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pass | `Turn the shaft assembly.` | article present |
| pass | `This data module tells you how to operate the unit.` | demonstrative plus article |
| pass | `Install the nuts (2) and the bolts (3).` | short sentence, article before each noun |
| pass | `Install the new O-rings (15), spacers (14), nut (13), and safety pin (12).` | one article: all items are new |
| pass | `Install the new O-rings (15), the spacers (14), the nut (13), and the safety pin (12).` | article on each: only the O-rings are new |
| fail | `Turn shaft assembly.` | article omitted |
| fail | `Data module tells you how to operate unit.` | two articles omitted |
| fail | `Tag the circuit breaker 36L7.` | article before an alphanumeric identifier |

## Notes

- The two "new O-rings" examples are both correct and mean different things. A
  checker must not normalize one into the other; it can only report that the
  article pattern of a series is ambiguous when an adjective is present.
- The standard points at "Practical English Usage" by Michael Swan for the article
  rules themselves, which is a fair signal that this rule is not fully decidable.
