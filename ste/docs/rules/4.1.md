# Rule 4.1 — Short and clear sentences

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-4-1)
> Write short and clear sentences.

- **Applies to:** every sentence, in procedures and in descriptions.
- **Needs:** judgment.
- **Decidable:** no as written. It is the principle that rules 5.1, 5.2, 6.1, 6.3,
  and 4.3 make measurable.
- **Code:** not implemented, and should not be: report it through its measurable
  parts.

## Discrete form

This rule has no independent test. Read it as an alias:

```
check(text, structure S) =
    5.1(text) ++ 5.2(text)          if S.mode == :procedural
    6.1(text) ++ 6.3(text)          if S.mode == :descriptive
    ++ 4.3(text)                    when the sentence lists many items or actions
```

## What the standard asks for

| Mode | Requirement | Measurable rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| procedural | give short and clear instructions directly to the reader, in the imperative | 5.1, 5.3 |
| procedural | one action per work step | 5.2 |
| descriptive | one topic per sentence, no imperative | 6.1, 6.5 |
| descriptive | give information about that topic gradually in the sentences that follow | 6.1, 6.2 |
| both | do not write abstract sentences; say what to do or how the system operates | judgment |

## Examples

| Verdict | Text | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fail | `To remove the cover assembly (9), first remove the four screws (10) that attach the cover (11) to the housing (12), and then, after taking the cover (11) off the housing (12), remove the preformed packing (13) and throw it away.` | one sentence, four actions → three work steps |
| fail | `The side stay assembly has two folding toggles hinged together and attached with hinges between the main gear strut and the side stay bracket.` | more than one topic → three sentences |
| fail | `No leaks are permitted.` | abstract: it does not tell the reader the action → `Make sure that there are no leaks.` |
| fail | `Different temperatures will change the cure time.` | abstract: no information about how → `When the temperature increases, the cure time will decrease.` |

## Notes

- The abstract-sentence half is the part that no other rule covers and that no
  checker can decide. It is worth keeping as a review question in a checklist:
  does this sentence tell the reader an action or a measurable fact?
