# Rule 2.2 — Long technical nouns

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-2-2)
> When a technical noun has more than three words, write it in full. Then, you can
> use one of these methods to make the technical noun clear:
> - Give a shorter form of the technical noun.
> - Use hyphens (-) between words that you use as one unit.

- **Applies to:** an official technical noun of more than three words, in the
  document where it occurs.
- **Needs:** glossary, document.
- **Decidable:** yes for the mechanics (first occurrence in full, later
  occurrences shortened), no for whether the short form is clear.
- **Code:** not implemented.

## Discrete form

```
check(document, glossary G) -> [finding]

for each official technical noun t of G with word_count(t) > 3 that the document
uses:
    occurrences = positions of t or of its short form
    if the first occurrence is not the full form:
        finding(t, reason: :not_written_in_full_first)
    if a later occurrence is the full form and a short form is defined:
        finding(t, reason: :shorten_after_first_use, use: G.short_form(t))
    if a short form or abbreviation occurs before it is introduced:
        finding(t, reason: :short_form_not_introduced)

pass = findings == []
```

## The two methods

| Method | Example |
| --- | --- |
| shorter form | `...engage the ramp service door safety connector pin (the pin that holds the ramp service door, referred to in this procedure as the "safety connector pin").` Then use "safety connector pin", which is three words and obeys rule 2.1. |
| abbreviation | `The Main Fuel Metering Unit (MFMU) is an aluminum alloy unit...` Then use "MFMU". |
| hyphens | `Inspection of the lavatory rapid-decompression device.` |

## Notes

- Three refusals the standard adds:
  - Do not abbreviate a technical noun of three words or fewer: write "the
    diaphragm assembly (8)", not "the DA (8)".
  - Do not hyphenate a technical noun of three words or fewer: "poppet valve
    assembly", not "poppet-valve assembly".
  - Do not remove a hyphen that the official technical noun has:
    "inward-outward valve" stays as it is.
- A text full of abbreviations is shorter and not easier to read, which the
  standard says directly. A checker can report the density of abbreviations in a
  procedure as a warning, separate from this rule's verdict.
