# Rule 8.2 — Hyphens

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-8-2)
> Use hyphens (-) to connect words that are directly related.

- **Applies to:** every hyphenated group, and every group that should be
  hyphenated.
- **Needs:** parser, judgment ("directly related" is a meaning test).
- **Decidable:** the five listed patterns can be recognized; whether two words are
  related cannot.
- **Code:** not implemented. `Ste.Text.hyphen_parts/1` gives the parts of a
  hyphenated word.

## Discrete form

```
check(text, glossary G) -> [report]

for each hyphenated group h of text:
    if G lists h as a technical noun:                     continue   # never change it
    if h matches one of the five patterns:                continue
    else: report(h, :check_that_the_words_are_related)

for each group of 4 or more hyphen-connected words:
    finding(h, :too_many_words_in_one_hyphenated_group)   # rule 2.1

pass = the second kind of finding fails a text; the first reports
```

## The five patterns of the standard

| # | Pattern | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | two or more words that are an adjective before a noun | low-altitude flight, high-pressure chamber, quick-release fastener, in-flight entertainment system, fire-resistant material, up-to-date information, soap-and-water solution, three-to-one ratio |
| 2 | two-word fractions or numbers | forty-seven, ninety-ninth, one hundred and sixty-two, three-sixteenths |
| 3 | an uppercase letter or a number plus a noun, giving a shape or configuration | L-shaped bracket, O-ring, U-beam, V-band clamp, 3-prong connector, 180-grit abrasive cloth |
| 4 | a verb whose first part is a noun or another part of speech | die-cast, arc-weld, stop-drill, vacuum-pack, heat-treat, short-circuit, dry-clean |
| 5 | a prefix that ends in a vowel before a root that starts with a vowel | pre-amplifier, de-icing, anti-icing, pre-engage |

## Examples

| Verdict | Text | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pass | `Make sure that the cutoff-switch power connection is safe.` | 3 words by the count of rule 8.7 |
| pass | `Move the main-gear-door retraction-winch handle.` | two hyphenated units plus the head noun |
| fail | `Move the main-gear-door-retraction-winch handle.` | one group of five words: the hyphens no longer show which words are related |
| fail | `Remove the diaphragm-assembly (8) from the valve body (20).` | a technical noun of two words needs no hyphen (rule 2.2) |
| fail | `The inward outward valve is part of the fuel system.` | the official technical noun is "inward-outward valve"; do not remove its hyphen |

## Notes

- A hyphen is not a dash. A dash divides ideas, shows a range, or marks a pause,
  and is often written as a hyphen with a space on each side, so a checker must
  ignore ` - ` when looking for hyphenated groups.
