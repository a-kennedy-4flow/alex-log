# Rule 1.4 — Forms of verbs and adjectives

**Statement** (Issue 9, page 1-1-4)
> Use only the approved forms of verbs and adjectives.

- **Applies to:** every verb and adjective of the text that the dictionary
  approves.
- **Needs:** dictionary (the forms of each entry).
- **Decidable:** yes. The dictionary lists every form it approves, so a form that
  is not listed is a finding; no tagger is needed to test membership.
- **Code:** not implemented. `Ste.Dictionary` already keeps the `forms` of every
  entry.

## Discrete form

```
check(text, dictionary D) -> [finding]

for each word w of text:
    if D approves w as a headword or as a listed form:      continue
    if w is the plural of an approved countable noun:        continue
    if w is a form of an approved verb or adjective that D does not list:
        finding(w, approved_forms: D.forms(stem(w)))

pass = findings == []
```

The third test is what makes this rule different from rule 1.1: `removing` is
built from the approved verb `REMOVE` but is not one of `REMOVE, REMOVES,
REMOVED, REMOVED`, so it fails here and not as an unknown word. The same holds
for a comparative that the entry does not print: adjectives that make their
comparative with "more" and "most" have no `-er` and `-est` form in the
dictionary, because "more" and "most" are approved words.

## What the dictionary approves

| Entry | Approved |
| --- | --- |
| `REMOVE (v), REMOVES, REMOVED, REMOVED` | (to) remove, remove(s), removed, and removed as an adjective |
| `GIVE (v), GIVES, GAVE, GIVEN` | (to) give, give(s), gave, and given as an adjective |
| `SLOW (adj) (SLOWER, SLOWEST)` | slow, slower, slowest |
| an adjective with no forms in parentheses | the base form, plus "more"/"most" + the base form |

## Examples

| Verdict | Text | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pass | `The operator removed the linkage.` | listed simple past |
| pass | `Examine all parts of the disassembled unit.` | listed past participle as an adjective |
| pass | `Make sure that the movement of the elevators is slower.` | listed comparative |
| fail | `While removing the cover, hold the panel.` | `removing` is not a listed form (see also rule 3.5) |
| fail | `Use the reddest indicator.` | colors have no comparative or superlative form (rule 1.5, category 17) |

## Out of scope

- Which tenses you may build from the approved forms — rule 3.2.
- Auxiliary verbs that build a complex construction out of approved forms —
  rule 3.4.
- The `-ing` form, which rule 3.5 permits only as a technical noun or as a
  modifier in one.
